## 5. Problem-Solving & Decision-Making
- Multi-currency accuracy: Write-time conversion, Decimal(15,2), locked exchange rates
- AI reliability: LLM + deterministic rule engine fallback, schema validation
- Security: JWT in HttpOnly cookies, bcrypt, KMS, Zod validation, no PII sent to OpenAI
- Scalability: Modular monolith, ECS Fargate, Aurora Serverless, stateless services

---

## 6. Key Learnings & Reflections
- AI is non-deterministic—always validate and have a fallback
- Design for scale from day one, even if you don’t need it yet
- Security and accuracy are non-negotiable in fintech
- Clear separation of concerns (routes, services, repositories) makes future changes easy
